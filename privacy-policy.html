Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This section explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

What we collect

We may collect personal information in the following circumstances:

  • When you submit an enquiry through our contact form your name, email address, contact number and the content of your message
  • When you download a resource from this Website - your name and email address
  • When you register for a workshop or programme your name, email address, and any additional details requested in the registration form

We do not collect sensitive personal data beyond what you voluntarily provide, and we do not use tracking cookies beyond what is necessary for basic website functionality.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide solely to

  • Respond to your enquiry or message
  • Deliver the resource you have requested
  • Process and confirm your workshop or programme registration
  • Share relevant updates about workshops, programmes, or resources only where you have indicated your interest or given consent.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How we store your information

Any information you share with us is stored securely. We retain your information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to any legal obligations we may have to retain it
  • Withdraw consent for us to contact you at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please write to us at team@leadwithceva.com.

Third-party services

This Website may use third-party tools including Calendly for scheduling and other platforms for resource delivery, workshop registration and processing payments. These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them. We are not responsible for the data practices of third-party providers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
